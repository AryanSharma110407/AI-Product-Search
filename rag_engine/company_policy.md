# Corporate Procurement Policy — TechNova Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2025
**Last Revised:** August 15, 2025
**Policy Owner:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

---

## 1. General Procurement Guidelines

All procurement requests must be submitted through the AI Procurement Agent system.
Purchases exceeding department-level autonomous limits require manager approval
before processing. Emergency purchases may bypass standard workflows with
retrospective approval within 48 hours.

---

## 2. Approved Hardware Manufacturers

The following hardware brands are pre-approved for procurement without additional
vendor verification:

- **Laptops & Desktops:** Dell, HP, Lenovo, Asus (different series only)
- **Monitors:** LG, Dell, BenQ, Samsung
- **Peripherals:** Logitech, Microsoft, Jabra
- **Networking Equipment:** Cisco, Ubiquiti, TP-Link (enterprise series only)
- **Servers & Storage:** Dell EMC, HPE, Supermicro

### Restricted Brands
- **Apple:** All Apple products (MacBooks, iMacs, iPads) require **CTO sign-off**
  regardless of price. Apple peripherals (keyboards, mice) are exempt from this rule.
- **Custom/White-label hardware:** Requires vendor security audit before procurement.

---

## 3. Department Budget Limits

### 3.1 Autonomous Approval Limits (Per Transaction)

| Department   | Autonomous Limit | Notes |
|-------------|-----------------|-------|
| Engineering  | Rs 80,000       | Hardware & software tools |
| Sales        | Rs 40,000       | Client-facing equipment |
| Marketing    | Rs 30,000       | Campaign materials & tools |
| HR           | Rs 25,000       | Office equipment |
| Operations   | Rs 50,000       | Infrastructure & facilities |
| Finance      | Rs 20,000       | Administrative tools |

### 3.2 Escalation Tiers

| Purchase Amount       | Required Approval          |
|----------------------|---------------------------|
| Below autonomous limit | Auto-approved by system    |
| Rs 80,001 - Rs 2,00,000 | Department Head approval  |
| Rs 2,00,001 - Rs 5,00,000 | VP/Director approval    |
| Above Rs 5,00,000     | CFO + CEO joint approval   |

---

## 4. Software & SaaS Procurement

- All SaaS subscriptions must be reviewed for **data privacy compliance** before approval.
- Annual subscriptions are preferred over monthly billing (minimum 10% savings required).
- Open-source alternatives must be evaluated before purchasing commercial software.
- Cloud infrastructure purchases (AWS, GCP, Azure) for Engineering are pre-approved
  up to the department's autonomous limit.

---

## 5. Vendor Payment Terms

- Standard payment terms: **Net 30 days** from invoice date.
- Advance payments exceeding Rs 1,00,000 require CFO pre-approval.
- All vendor contracts above Rs 5,00,000 must include an exit clause.

---

## 6. Financial Health Safeguards

### 6.1 Cash Reserve Policy
The company must maintain a **minimum cash reserve** equal to 2 months of
operating expenses at all times. Any purchase that would reduce the cash
balance below this threshold is automatically **BLOCKED** by the system.

### 6.2 Monthly Spending Caps
Each department's total monthly procurement spending (cumulative across
all transactions) should not exceed **3x their autonomous limit** without
VP-level review.

### 6.3 Quarterly Budget Reviews
Department budgets are reviewed quarterly. Departments that consistently
underspend by more than 40% may have their budgets reallocated.

---

## 7. Emergency Procurement

Emergency purchases (e.g., critical server failure, security incident response)
can bypass standard approval workflows under the following conditions:

1. The purchase is **immediately necessary** for business continuity.
2. The requestor documents the emergency justification within **48 hours**.
3. A retrospective review is conducted in the next management meeting.
4. Emergency purchases are capped at **Rs 3,00,000** without CFO involvement.

---

## 8. Asset Tracking Requirements

All hardware purchases above Rs 10,000 must be:
- Tagged with a unique asset ID upon receipt
- Registered in the company asset management system
- Assigned to a specific employee or department
- Subject to annual physical verification

---

## 9. Sustainability & Ethical Procurement

- Prefer energy-efficient equipment (ENERGY STAR certified where applicable).
- Avoid single-use or disposable electronics.
- E-waste from replaced equipment must be disposed through certified recyclers.
- Favor vendors with documented sustainability practices.

---

## 10. Policy Violations

Violations of this procurement policy may result in:
- Transaction reversal and cost recovery from department budget
- Suspension of autonomous procurement privileges
- Disciplinary action as per company HR policy

All procurement decisions are logged in the audit system for compliance review.
